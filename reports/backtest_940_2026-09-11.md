# Backtest: production 940-universe momentum strategy

_Run 2026-09-11. Read-only on data/*.duckdb; no bars fabricated._

## Setup

- Universe: 940 symbols from data/universe_symbols.txt (all present in DB)
- Data window: 2025-08-07 -> 2026-09-10 (275 trading days; ~2y requested, ~1.1y available)
- Strategy window: 2025-11-10 -> 2026-09-10 (209 days, 0.83 years)
- Rebalance: weekly at Friday close (45 rebalances); entry/exit at next open (deviation D1/D2 documented in script header)
- Signal: production score_frame composite (3.0*Vz + 2.0*M20 + 1.5*M60 + 1.5*H + 1.0*R) with gates (20d avg $vol >= $5M, price >= $2, 65+ bars), no lookahead
- Portfolio: top-40 -> max 2/sector -> top 10; stop = entry - 3xATR(14), target = entry + 6xATR(14) (2R)
- Sizing: $100k paper, shares = floor(1000 / (entry - stop)), 25% notional cap (D3)
- Costs: $1/fill + 5 bps slippage each way
- Exits: stop checked before target on ambiguous days; dropped names exit at next open; kept names hold original brackets
- Prices: unadjusted closes (splits/dividends not modeled); survivorship bias present (universe = today's large caps)

## Benchmark notes

- Equal-weight: all gate-passing symbols, rebalanced monthly at the close via the clean-room vectorized engine (1bp commission + 5bps slippage, both sides; deviation D5).
- SPY: Yahoo Finance daily closes 2025-08-01..2026-09-10, single buy at the first window close, $1 + 5bps entry cost, held.

## Verdict

The ranked top-10 strategy returned 13.5% (CAGR 16.5%, Sharpe 1.40) vs 16.5% for equal-weight and 11.2% for SPY, net of $1,571 in costs across 148 closed trades. It did NOT beat equal-weight net of costs — the composite ranking, sector cap, and 3xATR/2R brackets as implemented do not earn their turnover in this window. Do not trade this on real capital; the next step is diagnosing whether the signal, the sizing, or the exit rules are the leak.
